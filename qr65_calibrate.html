How to match

  1. In SignalRGB pick the Solid Color effect.
  2. Set it to white. If the speaker looks bluish, lower Blue strength; greenish, lower Green; pinkish, lower Red, until its white matches your other components.
  3. Try red, green and blue; each should look like the others.
  4. Try mixed colors like orange, purple or teal. If they lean towards one channel, raise that channel's gamma a little (above 1 = that channel weaker in mixes).
